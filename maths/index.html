Trigonometry

01

The Unit Circle

Drag a point round the circle: sin, cos, tan, sec, cot and cosec appear as real line segments and unroll into their graphs. Allied angles appear as reflections and turns, and the standard values come from three triangles.

Identities as Pictures

sin(A + B) from two stacked triangles, a sin x + b cos x as two arrows adding to one of length √(a² + b²), sum-to-product as two waves inside an envelope, and angle sums that close into a polygon.

Equations and Inverses

Progressions: Add or Multiply

The AP as a staircase that pairs with its flipped copy to give Sₙ = n(a + l)/2, and the GP as shrinking hops that settle at a/(1 − r). Also telling AP from GP, word problems, and AP × GP series as an area.

Means and Sums You Can See

AM ≥ GM ≥ HM inside one semicircle with a draggable point. Σk, Σk² and Σk³ = (Σk)² as block pictures, telescoping sums as gaps on a number line, and recurrences pulled towards a fixed point.
