Thermodynamics: the PV Diagram

Drag a process on the PV diagram and watch the gas in its cylinder: work is the area under the path, Q = ΔU + W as bars, cycles, path dependence, why Cp > Cv, and the physics vs chemistry sign conventions on one picture.

 04

Heat Engines and the Second Law

Hot source → engine → cold sink as bands whose widths add up. The two "impossible machines" (Kelvin–Planck, Clausius), a step-through Carnot cycle, and refrigerators as engines run backwards.

thermodynamics · second law · engines

 05

Kinetic Theory: Gas as Moving Molecules

A box of bouncing molecules whose wall hits make the pressure and the gas laws. Temperature as average kinetic energy, the speed distribution with vmp, vavg and vrms and why each sits where it does, Graham's law, mean free path, and when a real gas stops being ideal.

kinetic theory · speeds · mean free path

 06

Degrees of Freedom and Equipartition

Switch on each way a molecule can move (translation, rotation, vibration) for He, O₂, CO₂, H₂O and CH₄, and watch ½kBT energy buckets add up to Cv, Cp and γ. Gas mixtures, solids (3R), water, and why modes freeze when cold.

kinetic theory · equipartition · specific heats

 07

Heat Transfer: Conduction, Convection, Radiation

Heat flowing along a rod and through rods in series and parallel, ice growing on a pond, sea and land breezes, black-body spectra with Wien's and Stefan's laws, a thermos flask taken apart, and Newton's law of cooling.

heat transfer · radiation · cooling

 08

Temperature and Thermal Expansion

Drag one marker across °C, °F, K and a scale of your own. Extend gas-thermometer lines back to absolute zero. See why solids grow when heated, why area and volume expand by 2α and 3α, why water is densest at 4 °C, and what happens when expansion is blocked.

thermometry · expansion · thermal stress

Heat, Calorimetry and Change of State

Same heat, different warming. Drag your guess until heat lost equals heat gained. The heating curve from ice to steam drawn to scale, ice that doesn't all melt, phase diagrams of water and CO₂, cooking on hills and in a pressure cooker, and regelation.

calorimetry · latent heat · phase diagrams
