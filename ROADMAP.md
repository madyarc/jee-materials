# Roadmap — Class 11 topics not yet covered

Chapter list from the current NCERT Class 11 books (rationalised edition, reprint 2026–27). "Visual value" is how much a picture or interaction beats a textbook for that topic; ★★★ = build first. Before building any topic, list its items in that subject's `SYLLABUS.md` from the NCERT chapter text (see `CLAUDE.md`).

## Physics

Done: Ch 2–7 (all of mechanics), Ch 10–12 (thermal physics), Ch 13–14 (oscillations and waves), every ★★★ topic below. Remaining: Ch 8, Ch 9, Ch 1.

| NCERT chapter | What's left | Visual value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ✅ 2 Motion in a straight line | x–t, v–t, a–t graphs and how they turn into each other; area = displacement; free fall | ★★★ | Students mis-read graphs constantly; drag a velocity and watch all three graphs |
| ✅ 3 Motion in a plane | Vectors (add, resolve), projectile motion, relative velocity (river–boat, rain–man), uniform circular motion | ★★★ | Pure geometry; projectile and relative-velocity pictures settle most doubts |
| ✅ 4 Laws of motion | Free-body diagrams, static vs kinetic friction, pulleys and connected bodies, banking of roads, circular motion | ★★★ | FBDs are the core JEE skill and are visual by nature |
| ✅ 5 Work, energy and power | Work as area under F–x, potential-energy curves (equilibrium, turning points), springs, power, collisions (elastic/inelastic, CM frame) | ★★★ | PE-curve "ball on a hill" and collision animations are very clarifying |
| ✅ 6 System of particles (rest) | Centre of mass, motion of CM, torque and cross product, angular momentum and its conservation, equilibrium of rigid bodies | ★★★ | Torque/lever and spinning-skater pictures; finishes the chapter |
| ✅ 7 Gravitation | Kepler's laws (equal areas), g with height and depth, gravitational PE, escape speed, orbits and satellite energy | ★★★ | Orbits drawn to scale; the "equal areas" sweep is the classic visual |
| ✅ 13 Oscillations | SHM as the shadow of circular motion, x/v/a phase relations, energy exchange, spring and pendulum | ★★★ | The circle-shadow picture is the whole intuition for SHM |
| ✅ 14 Waves | Transverse vs longitudinal, travelling wave y(x, t), wave speed, superposition, reflection, standing waves on strings and in pipes, beats | ★★★ | Moving waves are impossible in a book and easy on screen |
| 9 Mechanical properties of fluids | Pressure and Pascal's law, buoyancy, streamline flow, continuity, Bernoulli, viscosity and terminal velocity, surface tension and capillarity | ★★☆ | Bernoulli and capillary rise visualise well; some parts are definitions |
| 8 Mechanical properties of solids | Stress–strain curve, Hooke's law, Young's/bulk/shear moduli, elastic energy | ★★☆ | Stress–strain curve and the three kinds of deformation; short chapter |
| 1 Units and measurements | Dimensional analysis, significant figures, errors | ★☆☆ | Mostly rules; a small dimension-checker could help |

## Chemistry

Done: Ch 3 (periodic trends), Ch 8 (electronic effects, mechanisms), thermodynamics shared via physics.

| NCERT chapter | What's left | Visual value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Structure of atom | Bohr model and hydrogen spectrum (series as energy-level jumps), de Broglie, quantum numbers, orbital shapes (s, p, d), nodes, aufbau / Hund / Pauli filling | ★★★ | Orbitals and energy-level jumps are the textbook case for graphics |
| 4 Chemical bonding | VSEPR shapes (3D, rotatable), hybridisation, sigma/pi overlap, MO diagrams and bond order (O₂ paramagnetism), dipole moments, hydrogen bonding | ★★★ | 3D shapes and MO filling are hard to see on paper |
| 6 Equilibrium | Dynamic equilibrium, Q vs K, Le Chatelier (push the system, watch it respond), pH scale, weak acids/bases, buffers, common-ion effect, Ksp | ★★★ | Le Chatelier and buffers are intuitive once animated; heavy JEE weight |
| 5 Thermodynamics (chemistry-only parts) | Enthalpy, Hess's law as energy cycles, bond enthalpies, Born–Haber cycle, entropy, Gibbs energy and spontaneity (ΔH–TΔS map) | ★★★ | Energy-cycle diagrams; links to the shared physics pages |
| 9 Hydrocarbons | Conformations (Newman projections, energy vs rotation angle), cis/trans, Markovnikov and peroxide effect, aromaticity (Hückel), electrophilic substitution and directing groups | ★★☆ | Conformational energy curve is ideal; parts extend the existing mechanism player |
| 8 Organic: basic principles (rest) | Isomerism map, nomenclature, purification and analysis methods | ★★☆ | Isomer tree helps; purification is mostly descriptive |
| 3 Periodicity (rest) | Ionic radii, isoelectronic series, valence and oxidation states | ★★☆ | Extends the existing periodic table |
| 1 Some basic concepts | Mole concept, limiting reagent, concentration terms, empirical formula | ★★☆ | Particle-counting pictures for moles and limiting reagent |
| 7 Redox reactions | Oxidation numbers, balancing (half-reaction method), electrode processes | ★☆☆ | Mostly procedural; a step-through balancer could help |

## Mathematics

Done: Ch 3 Trigonometric functions (plus Class 12 inverse trig), Ch 8 Sequences and series.

| NCERT chapter | What's left | Visual value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 Conic sections | Circle, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola as slices of a cone and as focus–directrix curves; eccentricity slider; reflection property | ★★★ | Eccentricity morphing one conic into another is the key picture |
| 12 Limits and derivatives | Limit as approach, secant → tangent, standard limits (sin x / x), derivative rules seen on graphs | ★★★ | Secant sliding into a tangent is the definition of the derivative |
| 2 Relations and functions | Domain/range on graphs, graph transformations (shift, stretch, reflect), standard function graphs | ★★★ | Transformations by sliders; foundation for all of calculus |
| 4 Complex numbers | Argand plane, modulus/argument, multiplication as rotate-and-scale, conjugate as reflection | ★★★ | "Multiply = rotate" is a picture, not a formula |
| 9 Straight lines | Slope, the forms of a line, angle between lines, distance from a point to a line | ★★☆ | Drag two points and see every form update |
| 6 Permutations and combinations | Counting trees, arrangements vs selections, circular arrangements | ★★☆ | Trees and boxes make "order matters" visible |
| 14 Probability | Sample spaces as grids, events as regions, addition rule | ★★☆ | Two-dice grids and Venn regions |
| 7 Binomial theorem | Pascal's triangle, (a + b)ⁿ as area/volume, general term | ★★☆ | Pascal pattern and the square/cube picture |
| 13 Statistics | Mean deviation, variance and SD as spread | ★★☆ | Dot plots with draggable data points |
| 11 Introduction to 3D geometry | Octants, coordinates, distance formula | ★☆☆ | A rotatable axis frame; short chapter |
| 1 Sets | Venn diagrams and operations | ★☆☆ | Simple, but quick to do |
| 5 Linear inequalities | Solutions on the number line | ★☆☆ | Small topic |
